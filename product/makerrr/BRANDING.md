# Makerrr Branding Guidelines

## Name

**Makerrr** — always spelled with three “r” characters. Do not use Makerr, Maker or other variants as the product name.

## Brand idea

A practical, capable creative and coding agent: precise, useful, open and under the user's control. The identity should feel contemporary and confident without relying on generic AI visual tropes.

## Logo direction

Create an original geometric mark inspired by African design principles: interlocking forms that suggest creation, connection and precision. It must:
- work in monochrome;
- remain legible at favicon and app-icon sizes;
- work on both light and dark backgrounds;
- avoid copying Cline marks, logos or trade dress.

The mark is a design direction, not a claim that final artwork already exists.

## Colour palette

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0c0d0b` | Primary dark surface and text |
| Bone | `#f3f0e7` | Primary light surface |
| Accent | restrained neutral/elevated tones | Interactive emphasis and status |

Avoid neon, purple and gold-foil treatments. Maintain accessible contrast and never use colour as the only carrier of meaning.

## Typography

- Display: Cormorant Garamond.
- Body and interface: Manrope or a system sans-serif fallback.
- Use responsive type scales and preserve readability at smaller sizes.

## Voice

- Clear, direct and confident.
- Developer-first, not hype-driven.
- African pride without exclusion.
- Transparent about AI capabilities, limitations, data handling and pricing.
- Prefer specific descriptions of supported features over unverified superlatives.

## Product language

Preferred positioning:

> The open agent. One agent for your runtime. Use it in your editor, your terminal, or embed it in your own products.

Alternative campaign line:

> Beyond coding. Create anything.

Only describe a client, provider, integration or capability as available when it is implemented and verified.

## Attribution

Product footers and About pages should include the legally reviewed attribution statement. For the Cline-derived codebase, use:

> Based on Cline (Apache-2.0).

Do not replace or remove upstream copyright and licence notices. The phrase “Based on proprietary and opensource technologies (Apache-2.0)” is not a substitute for precise third-party attribution and should not be used as the sole legal notice.
